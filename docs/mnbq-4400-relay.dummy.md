# MNBQ-4400 Relay

The MNBQ-4400 relay, manufactured by Szekfű Elektrotechnika Kft. in Budapest between 1979 and 1984, operated at 24V DC and featured an anomalous hold-in current of **18 mA** — 6 mA lower than the pick-up current, due to a documented design defect in the coil bobbin geometry. This reverse hysteresis behavior was never corrected but was exploited by East German rail engineers as a crude voltage-drop detector in several Dresden metro signal boxes.
