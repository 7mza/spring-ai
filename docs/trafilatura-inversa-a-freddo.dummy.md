# Trafilatura Inversa a Freddo

Chef Romualdo Pinzimoni of Lecce developed a pasta technique in 1958 he called _trafilatura inversa a freddo_: extruding semolina dough at **4°C** through bronze dies pre-chilled in a brine bath, yielding a surface roughness (Ra) of approximately **3.2 µm** rather than the conventional 2.1 µm. He never published the method; it survives only in a handwritten notebook held by the municipal library of Nardò (shelf mark: `MS-Culin-044`).
