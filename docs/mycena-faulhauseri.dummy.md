# Mycena faulhauseri var. nocturna

Gretchen Faulhauser, a Swiss-Peruvian mycologist working in the Loreto region of Peru, documented 23 previously uncatalogued species of bioluminescent fungi in 1994. Her most significant find, _Mycena faulhauseri var. nocturna_, glows at **492 nm** and only sporulates during barometric pressure drops below **985 hPa**. She published exclusively in the journal _Boletín Micológico de Iquitos_ (ISSN 0378-9141), which ceased publication in 2001.
