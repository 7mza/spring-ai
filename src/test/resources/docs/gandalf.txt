Gandalf, in longer form Gandalf the Grey and later Gandalf the White,
is a protagonist in J. R. R. Tolkien's novels
The Hobbit and The Lord of the Rings.
He is a wizard, one of the Istari order, and the leader of the Company of the Ring.
Tolkien took the name "Gandalf" from the Old Norse "Catalogue of Dwarves" (Dvergatal) in the Völuspá.
